    \caption[The three-stage pipelining of the offload]{The three-stage pipelining of the offload. While the tiles
    process batch $b_i$, the host is already encoding $b_{i+1}$ into the
    second buffer and merging the results of $b_{i-1}$. Because the three
    stages overlap, the total time is not their sum but the
    product of the number of batches and the duration of the widest stage. In this
    implementation that stage is host encoding: only
    it executes without interruption, while the other two remain idle for part of each
    period. No further speed-up of the AIE core can therefore
    reduce the total time.}
