    \end{tikzpicture}
    \caption[The parallelism limit of map-reduce]{The parallelism limit of map-reduce for large k. Each thread
    maintains its own heap of size k and can reject elements only
    after the heap fills, i.e., only after it has seen at least k elements. When
    the chunk assigned to each thread is larger than k (a), the threshold
    engages early and the rest of the chunk is filtered. But when
    the number of threads increases enough that the chunk becomes smaller than k
    (b), no heap ever fills and filtering never engages.
    This is a property of the algorithm, not the implementation: neither the grid
    size nor the workspace layout can remove it.}
